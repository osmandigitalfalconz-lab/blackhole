How to download a video

  1. Copy the link. Open the video in its app or browser and tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Paste it and pick a quality. Drop the link in the box on the home page, then choose Best, 720p, 480p, or MP3 audio.
  3. Save the video. Click Download, then Save video when it is ready. Higher resolutions are merged into a single MP4 automatically.

Multiple links at once

Click “＋ Multiple links” under the download box and paste one link per line (up to 20). Each video is fetched in turn and gets its own download buttons.

Tips

  • Only public videos work. Private, deleted or login-only videos cannot be fetched.
  • If a link fails, check it opens in your browser first, then try again — platforms sometimes rate-limit requests.
  • MP3 is the right choice when you only need the audio.
