Privacy Policy

No account, no personal data

BlackHole needs no account and no sign-up. We do not ask for personal information, and we do not sell or share any data about you.

Links you paste

A pasted link is only used to fetch that video. Files are temporary: they are removed from our server after they are sent to you.

Analytics and cookies

We may use basic analytics (such as Google Analytics) to understand overall usage. Analytics may set cookies in your browser. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Third parties

Video pages are fetched from the platform that hosts them. Those platforms have their own privacy policies, which apply when their content is fetched.
